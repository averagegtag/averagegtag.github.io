Petrified Primates

Petrified Primates is a VR horror fangame where you run through an abandoned research facility while escaping aggressive, mutated baboons. Sprint, hide, and survive as these creatures stalk every dark hallway.

About the Game

Petrified Primates is a brand-new, Scary Baboon inspired game by AverageGTAG. Run around in dark facility hallways, escaping monsters and having fun!

Status

In development, so far we have multipler, working monsters, cosmetics.

Controls

Swing your arms to run, climb, and jump like a gorilla. Use momentum from your swings to navigate the facility and escape the mutated baboons.
